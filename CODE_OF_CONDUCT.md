# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes careful, practical feedback.

Please:

- keep technical and localization discussion respectful
- criticize translations, terminology, and tooling decisions constructively
- explain context when reporting wording, lore, or rendering problems
- avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or dogpiling
- do not publish private information
- do not spam ROM, piracy, download, BIOS, save, or commercial-asset material

Direct criticism of German wording, lore consistency, terminology choices, and textbox/rendering quality is welcome when it is specific and useful.

Moderation decisions are made to keep the project safe, focused, and legally conservative.
